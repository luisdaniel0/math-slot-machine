**Subject:** Slot game art + animation — celestial theme, maths finished, ready for art

Hi [name],

I'm building **Starwake**, a 5×4 slot for **Stake Engine** (Stake.com's platform for
independent studios), and I'm looking for an artist to define its look.

**The idea.** Winning lines trace a constellation onto the reels, one star at a time. When
the shape completes, the constellation wakes — and a **beast made of stars**, a 2×2
creature, steps onto the board and walks around it for the rest of the round, swallowing
falling stars and growing a multiplier that pays every line it touches. Four of them:
a crow, a bear, a dragon, and a rarer ascended dragon.

**What I need.** Symbol set (~11 symbols), four constellations, three beast designs with
walk cycles, backgrounds, and the UI for a six-option buy menu. Animation matters as much
as static art here — the beast walking and feeding is the game.

**Where the project is.** The maths is **finished, tested and published** — payout
structure, constellation shapes and the full event list are locked and won't move under
you. That means no redesigns because someone changed a number. What isn't decided is the
art direction, and that's genuinely open: I have a theme, not a style.

**One moment worth knowing about.** There's a rare event where the sky starts raining much
larger stars mid-round — it's the only route to the game's 25,000× max win, and it has no
art at all yet. If something about that idea appeals to you, we'll get on well.

**If you're interested,** I'd love to see your portfolio (slot or casino work is a plus but
not essential), a quick read on how you'd approach the theme, and rough scope/timeline.
Happy to send the full brief with the complete asset and animation list.
